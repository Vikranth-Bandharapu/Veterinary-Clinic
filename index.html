Pet Wellness

Routine wellness checkups create a lifetime of joy.

Pets age more rapidly than humans, meaning subtle physiological shifts can occur within months. Comprehensive biannual or annual exams reveal silent conditions early.

Nose-to-Tail Physical Exam Cardiac auscultation, lymph node palpation, eye pressure checks, and musculoskeletal evaluation.
Body Condition & Nutrition Tracking Guiding ideal caloric intake, body condition scoring (BCS), and joint mobility adjustments.
Baseline Organ Function Testing Establishing healthy biometric baselines to spot kidney, liver, or thyroid trends before symptoms develop.
Explore Wellness Protocols
Immunity & Prevention

Shielding pets against contagious diseases.

Vaccination plans are never one-size-fits-all. We evaluate your pet's lifestyle, travel habits, wildlife contact, and age to select necessary immunizations.

Canine Core Immunity

Rabies, Distemper, Adenovirus, Parvovirus, and Parainfluenza (DHPP). Non-core options include Bordetella, Lyme, and Leptospirosis.

View Canine Schedule

Feline Core Immunity

Feline Viral Rhinotracheitis, Calicivirus, and Panleukopenia (FVRCP), along with Rabies. Feline Leukemia (FeLV) recommended for outdoor risk.

View Feline Schedule

Parasite Prevention

Year-round defense against heartworms, intestinal parasites (roundworms, hookworms), ticks, and fleas to protect pets and owners.

Explore Parasite Care
Veterinary Consultation Note: Vaccine protocols require individualized clinical assessment by a licensed veterinarian. Animals with previous vaccine sensitivities or specific autoimmune histories receive tailored antibody titer testing.
Diagnostics & Lab

Precise diagnostic answers for focused care.

PawCare provides core in-house diagnostics alongside reference laboratory partnerships to assist clinicians in uncovering the underlying causes of pet illness.

Digital Radiography (X-Ray) Rapid imaging for skeletal assessments, abdominal obstructions, and cardiopulmonary evaluations.
Ultrasonography Imaging Non-invasive soft-tissue evaluations of abdominal organs, kidneys, bladder, and guided aspirates.
Point-of-Care Laboratory Complete blood counts, serum chemistry, urinalysis, electrolyte analysis, and cytological cytology.
