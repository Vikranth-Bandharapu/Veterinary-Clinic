Visit Preparation

What to bring to your appointment.

A small amount of preparation helps our veterinarians conduct a comprehensive examination with minimal stress and maximum accuracy.

Medical & Vaccination Records Past vaccination certificates, spay/neuter proof, adoption paperwork, or previous veterinary clinic invoices.
Current Medications & Diet Photos Prescription bottles, supplements, or simply snap a clear phone photo of food ingredient panels and treat packaging.
Secure Carrier or Fixed 6ft Leash All feline companions in sturdy, well-ventilated carriers with familiar blankets; dogs secured on non-retractable leashes.
Fresh Stool Sample (If Requested) A small, clean sample collected within 4 hours for routine annual fecal centrifugation parasite screening.

First-Time Visitors

Tips for keeping your companion relaxed.

If your companion experiences car travel nervousness, motion sickness, or lobby anxiety, simple steps before departing home transform the entire experience into a calm visit.

Skip Heavy Breakfast on Exam Morning Slightly hungry pets are significantly more receptive to our high-value clinic treats (peanut butter, squeeze cheese, chicken broth).
Pheromone Calming Carrier Towels Lightly mist a clean towel with Feliway (for cats) or Adaptil (for dogs) 15 minutes before travel to reduce sensory stress.
Curbside Waiting for Reactive Dogs Call or text our front desk from your parking spot. We will guide you straight into a private exam room, bypassing lobby triggers entirely.
